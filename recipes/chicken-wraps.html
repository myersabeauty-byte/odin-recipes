Chicken Wraps

Home

Description

These delicious chicken wraps are a quick and easy meal packed with flavor.

>Perfect for lunch or dinner, they can be customized with your favorite toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound of breaded chicken tenders
  • 1 cup of shredded lettuce
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
  • 3 green onions, chopped
  • 8 (8 inch) flour tortillas
  • 1/4 cup stir fry sauce

Steps

  1. Slice chicken tenders into 1 inch strips. Set aside.
  2. In a large bowl combine lettuce, cilantro, green onions, and stir fry sauce and toss until it is well coated.
  3. Place tortilla on work surface and place a few strips of chicken in the center and top with salad mix.
  4. Fold the ends of the tortilla towards the center and roll. Repeat steps 3 and 4 with the remaining tortillas.
  5. Cut each wrap in half, and place on a platter ready to serve.

Pasta Salad

Home

Description

This refreshing pasta salad is the perfect dish for any occasion!

Packed with colorful vegetables, tender pasta, and a zesty dressing, it comes together in just minutes and is sure to be a crowd pleaser.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked elbow macaroni
  • 1 can whole black olives
  • 15 cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cup red wine vinegar
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons garlic powder
  • 1 1/2 dried basil
  • 3/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 cup sliced red bell peppers

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Whisk vinegar, garlic powder, basil, and black pepper together in a large bowl.
  3. Add cooked pasta, tomatoes, olives, and red peppers. Toss until evenly coated.
  4. Cover, and chill for 2 hours or overnight.
  5. Serve and enjoy!
